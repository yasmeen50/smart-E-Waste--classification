LAPTOP

What it is:
A laptop is a portable electronic computer containing a screen, motherboard, processor, memory, storage, and battery.

Recycling:
Take old laptops to an authorized e-waste recycling center. Many components can be recovered and recycled.

Reuse:
Repair the laptop, upgrade RAM or storage, donate it, or reuse it for basic tasks.

Safety:
Back up and remove personal data before disposal. Do not open or damage swollen or damaged batteries.

Environmental impact:
Improper disposal can release harmful substances. Recycling helps recover metals, plastics, and electronic components.

Disposal tip:
Never put laptops in regular household garbage.


MOBILE

What it is:
A mobile phone is a portable electronic device containing electronic circuits, a display, battery, camera, and other components.

Recycling:
Take old phones to an authorized e-waste collection or recycling center.

Reuse:
Repair, donate, sell, or reuse a working phone.

Safety:
Remove personal data. Do not puncture, crush, burn, or damage the battery.

Environmental impact:
Recycling recovers valuable materials and reduces electronic waste and pollution.

Disposal tip:
Do not throw mobile phones or batteries into regular garbage.


BATTERY

What it is:
A battery stores electrical energy and powers many electronic devices.

Recycling:
Always send used batteries to an appropriate battery or e-waste recycling collection point.

Reuse:
Only reuse rechargeable batteries when they are undamaged and suitable for the device.

Safety:
Never burn, puncture, crush, cut, or short-circuit a battery. Keep damaged or leaking batteries away from heat.

Environmental impact:
Improper battery disposal can cause pollution and fires. Recycling allows useful materials to be recovered.

Disposal tip:
Never put batteries in regular household waste.


CHARGER

What it is:
A charger supplies electrical power to recharge electronic devices.

Recycling:
Old chargers should be taken to an authorized e-waste recycler.

Reuse:
Working chargers can be reused with compatible devices that require the same specifications.

Safety:
Do not use chargers with exposed wires, broken plugs, or damaged insulation.

Environmental impact:
Recycling chargers helps recover metals, plastics, and electronic components.

Disposal tip:
Do not burn or throw chargers into regular household garbage.


KEYBOARD

What it is:
A keyboard is an electronic input device used to enter text, numbers, and commands.

Recycling:
Old keyboards should be taken to an authorized e-waste recycling center.

Reuse:
Working keyboards can be reused, repaired, donated, or used with another computer.

Safety:
Avoid using keyboards with damaged electrical cables or exposed wiring.

Environmental impact:
Recycling helps recover plastic, metals, and electronic materials.

Disposal tip:
Do not dispose of electronic keyboards with regular household waste.


PRINTER

What it is:
A printer is an electronic device used to print documents, photographs, and other materials.

Recycling:
Old printers should be taken to an authorized e-waste recycling center.

Reuse:
Working printers can be repaired, donated, or reused.

Safety:
Disconnect the printer from power before handling it. Handle ink or toner cartridges carefully.

Environmental impact:
Proper recycling reduces electronic waste and allows useful materials to be recovered.

Disposal tip:
Do not throw old printers into regular household garbage.
